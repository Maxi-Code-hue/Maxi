03 / CONCEPT

Form follows
feeling.

Villa Nova is a study in restraint. Built on the Bauhaus belief that form follows function, every wall earns its place and every opening frames a reason to pause. There is no ornament here — only proportion, light and material.

Three intersecting volumes are organised around a south-facing court. By day the house is washed in even northern light; by dusk it glows from within, a lantern set into the garden.

Concrete, oak and glass are left to speak for themselves. The result is a home that feels less designed than composed — calm, exact, and quietly radical.

Silence,
